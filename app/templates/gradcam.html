How Grad-CAM Works

Grad-CAM computes the gradients of the target class score yc with respect to the feature activation map Ak of the final convolutional layer:

αkc = (1/Z) ∑i ∑j (∂yc / ∂Ai,jk)

These gradients represent importance weights capturing the spatial locations most positively correlated with the predicted Alzheimer's category. The result is passed through a ReLU activation to retain only positive influences on the diagnosis.

Activation Heatmap Interpretation
Lowest Attention (Baseline) Highest Diagnostic Focus
  • Red & Yellow Hotspots: Regions with intense model attention. Typically identify ventricular expansion, temporal lobe cortical thinning, and hippocampal atrophy.
  • Green & Cyan Transitions: Intermediate activations corresponding to peripheral gyri and sulcal anatomical transitions.
  • Blue & Violet Areas: Background intracranial spaces and preserved non-degenerative tissue that do not drive the classification.
