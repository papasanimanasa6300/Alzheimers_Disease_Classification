Four-Class Alzheimer's Categorization

Rigorous deep learning classification trained on axial brain MRI scans.

Category 1
Non-Demented

Intact cortical volume, normal ventricular size, and healthy hippocampal preservation without detectable structural atrophy.

Category 2
Very Mild Demented

Early subtle cortical thinning and initial microscopic cellular degeneration; subtle medial temporal lobe changes.

Category 3
Mild Demented

Noticeable bilateral hippocampal volume reduction, widening of cerebral sulci, and mild lateral ventricular enlargement.

Category 4
Moderate Demented

Marked cortical atrophy, extensive enlargement of lateral ventricles, and pronounced neuronal degeneration.

Convolutional Neural Networks

High-performance deep CNN with Conv2D, batch normalization, dropout regularization, and softmax multi-class inference.

Explainable AI (Grad-CAM)

Gradient-weighted class activation mapping visualizes exact brain regions contributing to the neural network's predictions.

Cognitive Assessment

Multi-domain educational evaluation covering memory, attention, orientation, language fluency, and abstract reasoning.
